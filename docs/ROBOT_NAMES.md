# 🤖 The Master List of Fictional Robots, Androids & AI/Computer Systems

A research catalogue of famous fictional robots, androids, and artificial
intelligences from film, TV, novels, comics, video games, and anime — the
naming universe Alfred draws on for its **Robot Rating Ladder** (see
`RATING_SYSTEM.md`). Each is a candidate "rating tier" — e.g. *"this model earned
a 4¾ Johnny Five"* or *"a 3½ Chappie."*

Format: **Name** — _Work_ (year/medium) — one-line character note.

> Sourced from Wikipedia's *List of fictional robots and androids* and *List of
> fictional computers*, plus several "greatest fictional AI" rankings (links at
> the bottom), then curated. Marked ⭐ = strong, instantly-recognisable tier name.
